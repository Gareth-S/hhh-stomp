disconnect from your current Wi-Fi
 nmcli device disconnect wlp2s0

create the temporary hotspot
 nmcli device wifi hotspot ifname wlp2s0 ssid Hiphop-Hippy password hhh123456


find ip address
 nmcli device show wlp2s0 | grep IP4.ADDRESS


stop the hotspot
 nmcli connection down Hotspot

restart hotspot
 nmcli connection up Hotspot
